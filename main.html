How the lots fall

Translation — each drawn chapter is 80% likely to be NLT, and 20% likely to be a randomly chosen alternate translation (ESV, KJV, NIV, NASB, CSB, NKJV, AMP, NRSV, or MSG).

Selection — the pool starts in canonical (Genesis 1 → Revelation 22) order. The first of the three chapters is drawn uniformly at random from the pool. Each following chapter has a 50% chance of being the next chapter in the pool after the previous draw, a 10% chance of being the previous chapter in the pool, and a 40% chance of being a fresh random draw.

Password & drift — removing a chapter requires a password. Every time that password is entered correctly to remove a chapter, each chapter still in the pool has an independent 20% chance of swapping one place up or down. Immediately after the chapter is actually removed, a second pass gives each remaining chapter a 25% chance of the same swap.

The Ledger — every roll, swap, draw, removal, and import/export is logged with an ID below and in the DevTools console (window.LotAndLedger). State (lotAndLedgerState) and the running history (lotAndLedgerHistory) are saved separately in this browser's localStorage.
